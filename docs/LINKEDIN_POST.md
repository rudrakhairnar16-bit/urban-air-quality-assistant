🚀 Excited to unveil the **Urban Air Quality Assistant** — built natively with **Google Antigravity** for PromptWars Virtual Challenge 6!

Winter smog across Indian metros leaves millions breathing toxic air with little more than generic advice like "wear a mask" or "plant trees." We built an agentic, scientifically rigorous assistant that gives families quantifiable, physics-based control over their air.

### 🤖 Powered by an Antigravity 6-Agent Swarm:
• **Scout Agent**: Continuous background ingestion of CPCB station readings, OpenWeather, and satellite columns.
• **Analyst Agent**: Chemical Mass Balance (CMB) receptor model attributing PM2.5 across 6 distinct sources (traffic, stubble burning, industry, dust, open waste burning, secondary aerosols).
• **Strategist Agent**: 4-zone indoor physics engine (solving dynamic mass-balance infiltration, indoor cooking peaks, and filtration across bedroom, living room, kitchen, and bathroom).
• **Nudge Agent**: Thompson Sampling multi-armed bandit dynamically learning user compliance and optimizing messaging across 6 psychological framings (health, financial, social, urgency, simplicity, identity).
• **Sentinel Agent**: Persistent threshold monitoring armed with proactive alerts for forecast spikes.
• **Contributor Agent**: Zero-PII, k-anonymized (≥50 users per 1km grid) delta sharing to elevate neighborhood models.

### 📊 Quantified Personal & Community Impact:
Every single routine modification shows exact, actionable metrics:
• 🚌 **Metro commute:** Saves 2.3 kg CO₂/day, avoids 15 µg/m³ PM2.5 exposure, and saves ₹2,400/month vs driving.
• 🍳 **Cooking ventilation:** Exhaust fan + cross-ventilation cuts dinner PM2.5 peaks from 140 to 22 µg/m³.
• 👥 **Neighborhood Gaussian Plume Dispersion:** Simulates a 500m grid — if 30% of our block switches to metro, ambient PM2.5 drops by 12.5% for 340 neighbors in our 500m radius!
